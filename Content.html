Celestial Knowledge

Our solar system is just one of billions in the Milky Way galaxy. It consists of the Sun, eight planets, numerous
moons, asteroids, comets, and other celestial bodies. Each planet has unique characteristics that make it
fascinating to study.
Beyond our solar system lies the vast expanse of space, filled with countless galaxies, nebulae, and cosmic
phenomena that continue to amaze astronomers and space enthusiasts alike.

Amazing Astronomy Facts

Mind-blowing facts about our universe

  • A day on Venus is longer than its year
  • Neutron stars can spin up to 600 times per second
  • There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on Earth
  • One teaspoon of a neutron star would weigh about 6 billion tons
  • The Sun makes up 99.86% of the mass in our solar system
  • Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth
  • Footprints on the Moon will last for millions of years due to the lack of wind and water erosion
  • Light from the Sun takes about 43 minutes to reach Jupiter
  • The Milky Way galaxy is on a collision course with the Andromeda galaxy
  • Black holes can evaporate via Hawking radiation over extremely long timescales
  • Saturn’s rings are made mostly of ice particles with some rock and dust
  • The Boomerang Nebula is the coldest known place in the universe at −272°C
